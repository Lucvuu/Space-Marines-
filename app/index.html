Method (for re-checking)

Source code in core/eto.py, core/water_balance.py, core/rotation.py. Parameters and the source of every number are in config/crops.json and config/rotations.json.

  1. Reference evapotranspiration ETo: FAO-56 Penman-Monteith (Allen et al., 1998), computed daily from NASA POWER (T2M_MAX, T2M_MIN, RH2M, WS2M, ALLSKY_SFC_SW_DWN), minimum wind 0.5 m/s (FAO-56 p.63). The warmer scenario adds ΔT to both daily maximum and minimum temperature and keeps humidity unchanged.
  2. Crop water need: ETc = Kc × ETo. Kc from FAO-56 Table 12 (pp.111–112), interpolated by growth stage. Coffee uses the mid-season Kc for the whole dry season.
  3. Effective rain Pe: from monthly rain PRECTOTCORR; the rain scenario multiplies by (1 + Δ%). Rice follows FAO Training Manual 3 section 4.2: Pe = 0.8P − 25 when P > 75 mm/month, Pe = 0.6P − 10 when P ≤ 75. Upland crops and coffee follow USDA-SCS (the form used in FAO CROPWAT): Pe = P(125 − 0.2P)/125 when P ≤ 250 mm, Pe = 125 + 0.1P when P > 250.
  4. Extra water for rice (FAO Training Manual 3 section 4.4): land preparation 300 mm in the first month of the season (soil saturation 200 + standing water 100); a Winter–Spring rice crop sown after wet September–October months skips the 200 mm saturation; percolation 4 mm/day, lower bound 2 mm/day.
  5. Water to supply: for each month in the season, deficit = max(0; ETc + percolation + land preparation − Pe). Months are summed; a wet month does not offset a dry month because the model does not track soil water storage yet. The headline number is the mean over crop years 2015–2025; cards also show the median year and the dry year exceeded 1 year in 5. Unit conversion: 1 mm = 10 m³/ha = 1 m³ per 1,000 m².
  6. Satellite greenness: MODIS MOD13Q1 (250 m, 16-day) via ORNL DAAC, median of a 3×3 km window around the field point, keeping only pixels with reliability 0 or 1. Used only to cross-check the crop calendar, not in the water formula.
  7. Satellite cross-checks (shown next to the model, not used in the water formula): GPM IMERG monthly rain against NASA POWER rain; SMAP L4 dry-season soil moisture against the modeled deficit (Đắk Lắk); MODIS MOD09A1 flooding and greenness signals for observed rice sowing dates (Bến Tre); SRTM slope and published RUSLE C-factors for the cover-crop erosion comparison (Đắk Lắk).
  8. Not modeled yet: soil water storage, daily salinity dynamics, water and risk of the shrimp phase, yield, cost, market price.
